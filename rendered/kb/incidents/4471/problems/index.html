The three problems

parser — the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. Their dates come out with day and month swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug the #4471 page describes, and the queued fix is for it.

hint — the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant's way. The result looks like the parser bug, but the parser fix does not touch it. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants have it.

one-day — invites land one whole day off. These reports describe a shift by one day, not a swap: Tuesday shows as Monday, an invite arrives before the booking, two clients in different cities get different days for the same meeting. The desk already read them this way (desk-notes.json), but filed them under parser by tenant. A month/day fix cannot move a date by one day, so this needs its own ticket.

How it got merged

  • Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone. All three problems use the same words ("wrong day", "date is wrong"), so subject matching put them in one ticket.
  • Intake tags are a guess from the reporter's browser, and they disagree with the cause. Count by tenant, form and body, not by tag.
  • The export repeats some reports. Count each id once.
